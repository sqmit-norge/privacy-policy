# Deleting your data — Magic Coloring Doodle for Kids

## Request for Data Deletion

Thank you for reaching out regarding the deletion of your child's data.

To process your request, please send an email to info@sqmit.no, and we will attend to it promptly.

Please note that our kids' app does not require a login, so while we do hold data in our database, we are unable to directly identify which records belong to your child. It may therefore take some time to locate the relevant data. To help us verify ownership and ensure we delete the correct records, we may ask you for some additional details, such as:

- When your child first started using the app
- When your child last used the app
- The date and amount of your most recent payment

We appreciate your patience and will confirm once your data has been deleted.
