# Privacy Policy — Magic Coloring Doodle for Kids

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Magic Coloring Doodle for Kids ("we", "our", "the App") handles information. We built this App with children's privacy as a top priority.

## Our Promise to Parents

#### We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children.
The App:

- Does not require an account, sign-up, or login
- Does not collect names, email addresses, or birthdays
- Does not track or profile your child's activity
- Does not contain advertising of any kind
- Does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

## Information We Collect

The App saves your child's drawings and colored pictures in a secure database. This is done solely to allow retrieval of the drawings if you change devices. Additionally, we store this data to verify that your subscription is valid. The data is never used for any other purpose.

We want to assure parents that:
- The data is stored only to facilitate retrieval in case of device changes.
- The data is used solely for subscription validation purposes.
- The data is never shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

## Drawings and Saved Content

Any drawings or colored pictures your child creates are securely stored in our database. This ensures that you can retrieve them if you switch devices. The data is used only for this purpose and to validate your subscription. It is never shared with third parties or used for any other reason.

If you delete the App, you can contact us to request the deletion of all saved drawings from our database.

## In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases (for example, additional coloring packs). These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play, depending on your device. We never see or store your payment details.

Google: Payment information is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you have concerns about purchase-related data, please contact Google directly. We recommend enabling parental controls or purchase restrictions on your device to prevent unintended purchases by children.

## Children's Privacy Compliance

Because the App is directed at children, we comply with:

COPPA (the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — the App collects no personal information from children as defined by COPPA.
GDPR (the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation) — the App processes only minimal data (drawings and subscription validation) and does so in compliance with GDPR requirements.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted here with a revised effective date. We will notify parents of any material changes before they take effect.
